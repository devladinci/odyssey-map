# Roadmap

## What the map does today

| Part | State |
|---|---|
| Languages | English and Bulgarian, complete for every text; search works in both at once |
| Terrain | Real relief for the whole Mediterranean, sharper on Ithaca; 3D view; a view from Mount Neriton |
| Reader mode | A bookmark, 215 scenes for all 24 books, a camera that follows, 50 journeys that play on the map |
| Spoiler guard | Later books, later scene titles, the summary of the book being read, and the places and people Odysseus has not met yet stay hidden |
| The wanderings | Fourteen stops at their traditional places, each marked as disputed, with 12 other candidate locations |
| Journeys | Fifteen routes: Odysseus, Telemachus, Nestor, Menelaus, Hermes, and the journeys inside two tales |
| People | Cards for 29 characters in four groups, eight family trees, the gods by the side they take |
| Shape of the poem | A strip of the forty-one days of the action |
| Themes | The debate about the wanderings, kingdoms and returns, the suitors, archaeology, gods, winds, edges of the world, history, Linear B, later traditions, changing coasts |
| Honesty | Certainty marks, other candidate locations, schematic things labelled as schematic |
| Use | Links that hold the state, a remembered bookmark, offline use after the first visit |

## Open

| Part | Note |
|---|---|
| Review of flagged statements | See `content/REVIEW.md`. |
| A plan of the palace | The second half of the poem happens in one house. A schematic plan of the hall, the courtyard and the storeroom would help in Books 17 to 23. |
| The coast of about 1200 BC | The reconstructions carried over from the Iliad Map cover the Aegean. Pylos, the mouth of the Acheron and the Achelous delta are described in a tour but not yet drawn. |
| Rivers | The Alpheus, the Eurotas and the Acheron are not drawn. |
| Phones | 3D may be heavy on older phones. |
| One engine, two poems | This map and the Iliad Map share an engine but live in two repositories. They could become one site with a switch between the poems. |
| More languages | Copy `content/odyssey/text/en/`, translate the values, keep the keys and the spoiler tags, and add the interface words in `src/i18n.ts`. |
